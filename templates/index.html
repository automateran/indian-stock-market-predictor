METHOD

How this signal is formed

The consensus weighs the direction of the 20-session price trend first, then checks whether recent volume confirms the move. Rising price plus expanding volume produces a Strong Buy; a negative trend produces Sell.

RESEARCH NOTE METHODOLOGY

How it works

A transparent, rules-based reading of recent market behavior.

01

Look back 20 sessions

Each symbol is evaluated on the latest 20 daily candles, giving the signal enough context to distinguish momentum from a single noisy session.

02

Measure direction

The average of the most recent five closes is compared with the first five. This produces a simple trend percentage that is easy to audit.

03

Confirm participation

Recent five-day average volume is compared with the first five-day average. Strong Buy requires both upward price action and increased participation.

04

Label the consensus

Every outcome is surfaced as Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, or Sell with the inputs shown beside it. Nothing is hidden behind a black-box score.
